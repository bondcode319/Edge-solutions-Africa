# Content requiring legal or regulatory review

This site was written to avoid unsupported claims, but several areas touch regulated subject matter and need review by a qualified adviser before launch. Nothing here is legal advice, and no legal opinion, licence, approval or certification is asserted anywhere on the site.

While `NEXT_PUBLIC_LEGAL_REVIEWED` is unset, `/privacy`, `/terms` and `/cookies` display a visible notice saying they are unreviewed drafts.

## Priority 1 — regulated subject matter

| Topic | Where | What needs review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Green Points** | `/solutions/green-rewards`, `/`, footer, terms | The site states that points are not carbon credits, not a fixed quantity of CO2e, not cash, and carry no assumed transferability or guaranteed redemption. Confirm this matches the approved operating model, and whether the mechanism could be treated as a payment instrument, e-money, a voucher scheme or a loyalty scheme in each target market |
| **Project funding** | `/solutions/project-funding` | Donations, sponsorship and pre-purchases are described as not being investment products, with no promised returns. Confirm that pre-purchase arrangements as actually structured are not regulated investments or collective investment schemes in the relevant jurisdictions |
| **Payments** | `/solutions/payments-commerce`, footer, terms | The site states the company is not a bank, payment institution or licensed payment service provider and does not hold customer funds. Confirm this remains accurate as the model develops, and confirm the licensing position in each market |
| **Digital assets / blockchain** | `/platform#blockchain`, terms | Transferable-token functionality is described only as a future possibility subject to approvals. There is no token sale, price or investment framing. Confirm nothing on the site could be read as a financial promotion or offer of a digital asset |
| **Environmental claims** | Throughout | The site distinguishes platform records from independently issued carbon credits, states that the company does not issue credits, and never says a user has offset emissions. Review against green-claims and anti-greenwashing rules in target markets (including the phrasing of the "environmental value" layer) |

## Priority 3 — website and marketing

| Topic | Where | What needs review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Company legal identity in the copyright line and terms | Footer, `/terms` | No entity name, number or jurisdiction is stated yet |
| Forward-looking statements | `/terms` and every status badge | The site labels capabilities as concept/in development/planned and disclaims availability. Confirm the wording |
| Illustrative demonstration | `/platform#journey` | Every value is labelled simulated and the component states it is not connected to a real vehicle, operator, customer or registry. Confirm this is sufficient |
| Cookie and analytics disclosure | `/cookies` | Accurate today (no analytics provider). Must be updated if one is added, with consent handling where required |
| Draft articles | `/insights` | Drafts are labelled, undated, `noindex` and excluded from the sitemap. Approve before publishing |

## What the build team deliberately did not do

- Did not name any methodology, standard, registry or verification body as one the company works with.
- Did not state any jurisdiction as the governing law.
- Did not publish any emissions figure, impact statistic or usage metric.
- Did not describe any capability as available.
- Did not claim any partnership, licence, approval or certification.
