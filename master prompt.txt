17. SECURITY, PRIVACY, AND REGULATORY COMMUNICATION

The company strategy identifies regulatory, payment, data-protection, double-counting, and reward-funding concerns. The website must communicate the business responsibly.

Implement the following principles:

Environmental claims
Distinguish measured activity from verified environmental claims.
Distinguish platform records from independently issued carbon credits.
Avoid claiming that a user has offset emissions merely by earning points.
Avoid unsupported emissions-reduction statistics.
Avoid presenting illustrative calculations as verified results.
Rewards
Explain the Green Points concept accurately.
Do not claim points are transferable, cashable, or universally redeemable unless approved and implemented.
Do not promise a fixed point value or guaranteed financial benefit without approved terms.
Do not imply rewards are issued without appropriate funding.
Payments
Do not describe the company as a bank, payment institution, or licensed payment provider without verified authorisation.
Explain planned payment integrations as integrations with appropriate providers.
Do not imply that the company holds customer funds if it does not.
Tokens and blockchain
Do not launch a token sale page.
Do not add speculative token pricing.
Do not market the platform as a cryptocurrency investment.
Do not promise token appreciation, returns, or liquidity.
Present transferable-token functionality as a future possibility subject to the required legal and regulatory approvals.
Keep environmental measurement and verification conceptually independent of blockchain availability.
Data protection
Minimise personal data collection.
Avoid publishing raw GPS trails, passenger information, or personally identifiable operational data.
Provide appropriate privacy information.
Do not use real customer or vehicle information in demonstrations without permission.
Legal content

Build editable legal-information pages and configurable notices.

Do not invent legal opinions, licences, regulatory approvals, certifications, or compliance claims.

Mark content requiring legal review in the project documentation.
